Architecture Note
NVL72 Notes

NVL72 = half-rack variant of NVL144.
Same compute tray design (2 Superchips), simply half the count.

NVL72 was announced in production at CES 2026 (Jensen Huang, Jan 5 2026).
NVL144 volume production: H2 2026.

Cable-free midplane: assembly time cut from 100min (Blackwell) to 6 minutes per rack.
